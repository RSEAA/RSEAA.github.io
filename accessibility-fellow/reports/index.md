## Accessibility Fellowship

The Accessibility Fellowship aims to encourage individuals with disabilities - such as those who are deaf, hard of hearing, blind, have low vision, or have other disabilities - from within our community or adjacent communities to join the conference. Their involvement helps ensure we are held accountable for the accessibility of our online event.
